O a veces le cae a uno encima. Y ahí es fractura… asegurada.

Click to flip
Translation

Or sometimes it falls right on top of you. At that point it’s a fracture… guaranteed.

O a veces le cae a uno encima. Y ahí es fractura… asegurada.
Oh ah beh-ses | leh kah-eh | ah oo-no | en-see-mah | Yah-ee es | frahk-too-rah | ah-seh-goo-rah-dah
Spanish uses impersonal structure: “le cae a uno” = it falls on one.
Warning meaning a fracture is guaranteed if something heavy falls on you.
Contextual Vocab
  • a veces — sometimes
  • encima — on top (of)
  • fractura — fracture
  • asegurada — guaranteed
Quick Quiz
  1. What word means “on top (of)”? encima
  2. What word means “fracture”? fractura
  3. What word means “guaranteed”? asegurada
